TITLE: THE FIRSTBORN
SUBTITLE: The child who grew up before they were ready.

FORMAT:
Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 24fps.
Target duration: approximately 120–135 seconds.
Style: cinematic 2D animated short film, hand-painted backgrounds, soft watercolor textures, delicate film grain, natural character movement, atmospheric lighting.
Voice-over: Indonesian with one final English sentence.
Mood: intimate, melancholic, reflective, quietly comforting.

CORE STORY:
Tell the story of a firstborn child who grew up carrying responsibilities they never consciously chose. Explore the expectations placed on them, the quiet sacrifices, the guilt of choosing themselves, and the realization that being born first does not mean becoming a second parent.

The story must feel personal and human, not like a generic motivational advertisement. Avoid excessive crying, dramatic acting, forced optimism, preachy narration, and overused motivational symbolism.

VOICE-OVER STYLE:
Soft, mature, conversational Indonesian voice. Slightly tired but emotionally grounded. Natural pauses and breathing. Never sound like an advertisement, inspirational speaker, or dramatic poetry reading.
