# Curioverse

> Assumptions labeled: inferred from the user's brief and the Curioverse Brand Identity Guide (Oktober 2026), confirmed via one question round (names, theme, language register).

## Product truth
- **What it is:** personal general-knowledge website of a couple, AfreshiaLa & LeoFa. A home for "eh tau nggak sih" moments.
- **Niches:** Finance, Science, Sejarah, Biografi, Market, Saham, Global News.
- **Audience:** the two authors plus Gen Z readers who like learning random but real things. Read on phones at night and on laptops.
- **Voice:** Indonesian mixed with English, Gen Z register ("jujurly", "no cap", "deep dive"). Warm, curious, never preachy.
- **Primary job:** make a visitor start one story and fall into the next (rabbit holes), with Surprise Me as signature feature.
- **Constraints:** no invented news, no live market data, social posts close with the brand line "a curious mind lives here" instead of a disclaimer. Facts must be accurate and checkable.

## Brand commitments (pinned)
- Palette (Japan Club, aturan 60-30-10, sejak 5 Nov 2026): Cream #EFE4C6 60% (latar), Deep Space Navy #0B1450 30% (teks, band, kartu malam), Red #B80B17 10% (tombol, bintang, satu kata penegas). Dark mode: Deep Space #070B26 + cream + red #F0505A. Warna kategori tetap beda per kategori (content/categories.json), nuansa planet/nebula.
- Type: dua font saja. Instrument Serif (display; italic untuk catatan dan kata penegas) + Inter Tight (body/UI). Caveat dihapus.
- Motifs: celestial orbits, four-point stars, starfield tipis di permukaan navy, moon/rising-sun merah, monogram C, stamp seal.
- Light (Parchment) and dark (Midnight Ink) both designed; follows system with a working toggle.

## Surfaces
- `index.html`: Persuade/Read hybrid home + in-page reader. Mode: Read for articles, Persuade for the first viewport.
